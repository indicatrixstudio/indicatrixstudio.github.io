About

Developer & publisher of games that matter.

Martin Timothy Timko is a freelance programmer and teacher who creates computer games and applications in his free time.

  • I develop computer games using Unity/C#, and occasionally C/C++
  • I develop websites using ASP.NET/C# for the backend and React/Angular for the frontend
  • I develop applications mostly using .NET WinForms
  • Since January 2026, I have also been using AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude in my development process, while maintaining full control over the development and final result
  • I create some of my graphics myself, while others are sourced from the Asset Store, Synty, or AI. I also make minor modifications and adjustments myself
